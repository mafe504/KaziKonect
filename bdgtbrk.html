Kazi Konnect: Indonesia

Marketing & Growth Budget

Our Core Message

Kazi Konnect gives Indonesian schools a simple, powerful platform to teach and assess the critical soft skills students need for the future. We make it easy for teachers to integrate skills like critical thinking and collaboration into their existing curriculum, providing students with a digital portfolio that proves their abilities to universities and employers.

Indonesia's EdTech Market Opportunity

$3.2 Billion

(Projected to reach $8.8B by 2033)

Our Go-to-Market Strategy

We will use a mix of digital and offline strategies to build relationships with school administrators, teachers, and key educational partners.

LinkedIn

We'll target school administrators and NGO partners with professional case studies, data on the 21st-century skills gap, and articles on educational leadership.

Instagram

We'll engage teachers and parents with short videos of student projects, testimonials, and "Skill of the Week" graphics to build a community.

Offline: Teacher Workshops

We will host hands-on training and onboarding workshops to empower teachers, demonstrate value, and build strong school relationships.

Offline: NGO Partnerships

We will partner with educational NGOs and attend government conferences to reach public school decision-makers and secure grant-based funding.
